An opt-in `react` config, with two report-only rules for React state from the React books of `docs/catalog-react.md`, on `.jsx` and `.tsx` files only: `react-no-state-mutation` reports a `useState` or `useReducer` value changed in place (a member assigned or deleted, or a mutating array method such as `push` called on it), and `react-no-untyped-empty-state` a `useState([])` with no type argument in a `.tsx` file. `react` overrides no rule of `recommended`, so components and custom hooks keep `max-lines-per-function`'s 12 lines. And `react-extended`, which adds `react-hooks/rules-of-hooks` and `react-hooks/exhaustive-deps` (both `"error"`, on `.jsx` and `.tsx` files and on `use*.js` and `use*.ts` custom hooks) and `react/jsx-key` and `react/no-array-index-key` (on `.jsx` and `.tsx` only), listed one by one rather than through the plugins' presets. ESLint gets `configs.react` and `configs["react-extended"]`; Oxlint the `react` and `reactExtended` exports of `eslint-plugin-clean-code-book/oxlint`, where `reactExtended` runs Oxlint's native `react` plugin and loads no JS plugin. `eslint-plugin-react-hooks` (6 or later) and `eslint-plugin-react` (7.37 or later) are new optional peer dependencies, needed only by ESLint's `react-extended`. `recommended`, `recommended-type-checked`, `ai`, `node` and `node-extended` are unchanged.
